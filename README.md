# 🏫 CampusOps — Smart Campus Operations Management

**Transform campus complaints into actionable workflows. Track every issue from reporting to resolution.**

CampusOps is a campus operations management platform designed to simplify complaint handling and improve coordination between students, faculty, technicians, and campus administrators.

Instead of allowing complaints to get lost between departments, CampusOps provides a centralized platform to report issues, track progress, coordinate resources, and manage the complete resolution workflow.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Campus institutions often struggle with scattered complaints, delayed responses, limited follow-up visibility, and inefficient coordination between departments.

CampusOps addresses these challenges by organizing complaints into trackable incidents and structured workflows, helping campus teams respond efficiently and maintain accountability.

## ✨ Core Features

* 📝 **Complaint Management:** Submit and manage campus-related complaints.
* 🤖 **AI-Assisted Categorization:** Identify complaint types, locations, urgency, and affected assets.
* 🔗 **Duplicate Detection:** Connect related complaints to reduce duplicate work.
* 🛠️ **Technician Assignment:** Coordinate maintenance personnel and work orders.
* 📍 **Incident Tracking:** Monitor complaints from reporting through resolution.
* 🏫 **Room and Resource Management:** Identify scheduling conflicts and resource requirements.
* 📦 **Inventory Management:** Check part availability and identify shortages.
* ✅ **Approval Workflows:** Support purchase requests and authorized decisions.
* ⏱️ **SLA Monitoring:** Track resolution deadlines and escalation requirements.
* 📊 **Administrative Dashboard:** Provide visibility into incidents, workloads, and recurring problems.
* 🔐 **Role-Based Access Control:** Support permissions appropriate to different campus roles.

*These features describe the project's intended capabilities; the live demo may implement a subset of them.*

## 🔄 How CampusOps Works

1. **Report:** A student or faculty member reports a campus issue.
2. **Categorize:** The system identifies the issue type, location, and urgency.
3. **Correlate:** Related complaints are linked to an existing incident where appropriate.
4. **Assign:** The system identifies a suitable technician based on availability, skills, and workload.
5. **Coordinate:** Required rooms, parts, resources, and approvals are handled through the workflow.
6. **Track:** Progress, deadlines, and escalations are monitored.
7. **Resolve:** The completed work is verified, and the incident is closed with a record of its resolution.

## 🧠 Design Philosophy

CampusOps combines AI-assisted language understanding with deterministic business rules.

**AI helps interpret complaints. Rules govern assignments, permissions, resource availability, approvals, and workflow transitions.**

This approach supports structured decision-making, accountability, and reliable complaint handling.
